Technology Incubation Centre

Your idea deserves a bench.

Ready-to-use laboratories, scientific support and a community of people building bio-businesses in the Northeast. If your idea needs evidence, this is where it starts.

  • Flexible lab leases

The first milestone

A place to test the idea before you scale it.

The Technology Incubation Centre is the park’s core facility — a first home for researchers and founders who want to turn an innovative idea into a viable business.

Incubatees get ready-to-use laboratory space on flexible leases, shared office services and access to the park’s scientific facilities and expertise. That removes the upfront burden of building a lab and lets a small team validate its science, its product and its market.

Proposals are assessed on the merit of the innovation, the sustainability of the plan and the marketability of the product or service. Accepted teams join a working community of scientists, institutions and companies on one campus.

Who it is for

If the idea needs a lab, start here.

The centre is built for people at the point where an idea needs evidence — not for teams that already have a full facility.

01

Researchers

University and research-body scientists who want to develop their own results through a spin-off in Northeast India.

02

Young professionals

People with an innovative business idea who need laboratory space, scientific support and a credible place to start.

03

Companies

Established businesses diversifying into biotechnology or setting up a new R&D unit in the region.

What you get

Less building. More proving.

The point of incubation is to spend your time and money on the science and the market — not on setting up a facility.

Ready-to-use labs

Furnished modular wet labs with core and shell utilities, on flexible lease terms.

Shared services

Office support, library, training rooms, conference room, auditorium and cafeteria.

Scientific access

Analytical instrumentation, bioinformatics, bioprospecting and a scientific team on campus.

Confidentiality

Assurance of confidentiality and security for client data, materials and contractual results.

Business planning

Support with the business plan, funding routes and the commercial case around the science.

A wider network

Links to NRDC, NIDHI PRAYAS, BICFA, universities and research institutions across the region.

How to apply

Five steps from proposal to lab.

Applications are accepted on a rolling basis. The clearer the scientific and commercial case, the easier the review.

  1. 01

    Read the guidelines

    Download the application format and the information for applicants before you start writing.

  2. 02

    Prepare the proposal

    Describe the science, the product, the market, the space needed, the team, the funding and the facilities you will install.

  3. 03

    Submit to the CEO

    Send the completed proposal and annexures to the Chief Executive Officer by email or to the park address.

  4. 04

    Proposal review

    Proposals are assessed on innovation merit, sustainability and the marketability of the product or service.

  5. 05

    Agreement and move-in

    Accepted teams agree the lease and facilities, then move into the incubation centre.

What the proposal should cover

  • Space requirement, lease period and expected occupation date
  • Broad business plan, including the instruments required
  • Description of the promoter institution or company, with annual report or balance sheet
  • Means of funding
  • Number of staff likely to be based at the R&D centre
  • Major R&D facilities to be installed
  • Effluents, bio-risks and containment measures, with a commitment to regulatory compliance
