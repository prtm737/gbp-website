Technology Incubation Centre · Amingaon

Where the Northeast’s bio-science becomes business.

The region’s first biotech park — a joint initiative of the Government of Assam and the Department of Biotechnology, Government of India. Ready-to-use labs, scientific expertise and a community built to take ideas to market.

What we do

A launchpad, not just a park.

Three things have to line up for deep science to become a business: the right space, the right tools and the right network. We put all three on one campus.

Ready-to-use modular labs, flexible leases and shared office services — so a young company can start science without sinking capital into walls.

Focus areas

Nine sectors. One ecosystem.

From molecules to markets, the park is built for ventures that use the Northeast’s biological wealth in useful, commercial ways.

01

Healthcare & Pharmaceuticals

Therapeutics, herbal and natural-product medicines, fermentation products, immunodiagnostics, biosensors, vaccines, gene therapy and monoclonal antibodies.

02

Agro-Technology

Crop quality and yield, floriculture, forest tree species, plant molecular biology, biopesticides, biofertilisers and processed foods.

03

Environment

Bioremediation, microbial enhanced oil recovery, and the safe disposal of wastes and toxic materials.

04

Energy

Biofuels and renewable energy sources, including work on second-generation ethanol from agricultural waste.

05

Fisheries & Aquaculture

Hatchery and grow-out innovation, ornamental fish, biofloc systems and value addition through the BICFA centre.

06

Industrial Application

Drug intermediates, active pharmaceutical ingredients, plants as bioreactors, enzymes, catalysts, chemicals and biopolymers.

07

Food Processing

Molecular characterisation and value addition, shelf-life improvement, quality assessment and traditional processed foods.

08

Bioinformatics

Computational biology, data analysis and training supported by the park’s dedicated Bioinformatics Facility.

09

Plant Tissue Culture

Micropropagation, plant molecular biology and tissue-culture services for horticulture, forestry and commercial crops.
