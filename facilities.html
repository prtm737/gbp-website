Core facility

Technology Incubation Centre

The first milestone incubator at Guwahati Biotech Park — a place to test an idea, build a team and reach a market without taking on the full cost of a laboratory.

The centre offers fully furnished lab spaces with utilities and shared instrumentation, alongside office services and access to the park’s specialised facilities. It is designed for researchers, young professionals and companies setting up in the Northeast.

  • Fully furnished modular wet labs with core and shell utilities
  • Flexible leases for startups, researchers and companies
  • Shared office services, conference room and auditorium
  • Access to specialised analytical and bioprospecting facilities

Testing facility

Regional Honey
Testing Laboratory

Quality, authenticity and safety testing for honey and bee products — established with support from the National Bee Board under the National Beekeeping and Honey Mission.

Supported by National Bee Board Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India

What we analyse

CategoryParameters
Physical propertiesMoisture, specific gravity, electrical conductivity, water-insoluble matter
Chemical qualityAcidity, total ash, HMF, diastase activity, proline
Sugar compositionTotal reducing sugars, sucrose, fructose, glucose, F/G ratio
AuthenticityC4 sugar, C4 sugar δ13C, foreign oligosaccharides, SMR test
Botanical analysisPollen count and microscopic examination
SafetyHeavy-metal analysis

Methods include HPLC, LC-MS/MS, EA-LC-IRMS, spectrophotometric, gravimetric, titrimetric and microscopic techniques.

More on campus

Support that goes beyond the lab bench.

Computing, prototyping, fisheries, advisory, training and technology transfer — each facility connects to the same campus and the same community.

Computing

Bioinformatics Facility

Dedicated computing and analytical support for genomics, proteomics and data-driven biology, with training for students and industry teams.

  • Dedicated bioinformatics workstations and software
  • Requisition-based access for projects and dissertations
  • Hands-on training in the basics of bioinformatics
Prototyping

NIDHI PRAYAS

A pre-incubation and prototyping centre under the national NIDHI-PRAYAS programme, helping innovators move from a drawing to a working proof.

  • Fab-lab and fabrication support for physical prototypes
  • Mentorship on design, testing and validation
  • A bridge into the park’s incubation ecosystem
Fisheries

BICFA

The Business Incubation Centre for Fisheries and Aquaculture supports founders working across fish, aquaculture and allied value chains.

  • Biofloc and aquaculture demonstration units
  • Hatchery, breeding and ornamental fish training
  • Fish processing and value-addition programmes
Advisory

Consultancy Cell

Scientific and technical advice for entrepreneurs, industry and institutions — from method development to business-plan preparation.

  • Sample testing, analysis and method development
  • Instrument-use services, short and long term
  • Business-plan preparation and incubation advisory
Learning

Training & Exposure Visits

Hands-on programmes on sophisticated instruments and applied biotechnology, plus internships, dissertations and guided campus visits.

  • Instrument and technique-based hands-on training
  • Internship, project and dissertation placements
  • Exposure visits for schools, colleges and universities
Technology transfer

North East Cell of NRDC

The North East cell of the National Research Development Corporation connects regional innovators with technology transfer and IP support.

  • Technology transfer and commercialisation support
  • Linkages with national research institutions
  • IP and technology-scouting guidance

  • Value-added products, marketing and enterprise skills
