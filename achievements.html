The record

Photos and videos from the park.

Training cohorts, laboratory development, outreach drives and incubatee milestones — browse the moments that make up the work at Amingaon.

20 May 2026

Regional Honey Testing Laboratory serves beekeepers and FPOs

The on-campus RHTL supports beekeepers, farmer producer organisations and food businesses with quality, authenticity and safety testing, including sugar-profile and heavy-metal analysis.

Regional Honey Testing Laboratory, Amingaon

15 Jul 2025

Arbor Day tree-planting drive on the Amingaon campus

Staff, incubatees and guests joined a campus-wide plantation drive, adding native species to the green cover around the Technology Incubation Centre.

Guwahati Biotech Park campus, Amingaon

25 Jul 2026

Bio-entrepreneurial skills training and internship programme

A five-day programme for undergraduate and postgraduate students from across Assam, combining laboratory exposure with the practical side of building a bio-venture.

Technology Incubation Centre, Amingaon

10 Feb 2026

NIDHI PRAYAS innovators move from drawing to prototype

Pre-incubation teams used the park’s prototyping and fab-lab support to test designs, validate assumptions and prepare for the next stage of product development.

NIDHI PRAYAS centre, Amingaon

12 Jan 2026

Thirteen ventures working from one campus

From genomics and enzymes to mead, medtech and fisheries, the park’s incubatee community now spans thirteen resident companies and institutions.

Technology Incubation Centre, Amingaon

5 Mar 2026

Student exposure visits and hands-on instrument training

School, college and university groups visited the park for guided laboratory sessions on analytical instruments, bioinformatics and applied biotechnology.

Guwahati Biotech Park campus, Amingaon
