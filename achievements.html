The record

Photos and videos from the park.

Training cohorts, laboratory development, outreach drives and incubatee milestones — browse the moments that make up the work at Amingaon.

11 Sept 2024

PMMSY’s 4th anniversary and the BICFA story

Guwahati Biotech Park joined the national celebration of four years of the Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana, the programme funding the park’s fisheries and aquaculture incubation centre.

Sushma Swaraj Bhawan, New Delhi

10 Jan 2025

International Biomanufacturing Summit 2025

GBP was an organising partner at the summit hosted with NEISED and Gauhati University, and welcomed DBT senior advisers for a working session with regional entrepreneurs.

Gauhati University, Guwahati

30 Nov 2024

GBP at the India International Science Festival 2024

The park joined the State’s science and technology showcase at IIT Guwahati, with Dr Bula Choudhury speaking on innovation, technology and the start-up mission.

IIT Guwahati

20 May 2026

Regional Honey Testing Laboratory serves beekeepers and FPOs

The on-campus RHTL supports beekeepers, farmer producer organisations and food businesses with quality, authenticity and safety testing, including sugar-profile and heavy-metal analysis.

Regional Honey Testing Laboratory, Amingaon

15 Jul 2025

Arbor Day tree-planting drive on the Amingaon campus

Staff, incubatees and guests joined a campus-wide plantation drive, adding native species to the green cover around the Technology Incubation Centre.

Guwahati Biotech Park campus, Amingaon

25 Jul 2026

Bio-entrepreneurial skills training and internship programme

A five-day programme for undergraduate and postgraduate students from across Assam, combining laboratory exposure with the practical side of building a bio-venture.

Technology Incubation Centre, Amingaon

10 Feb 2026

NIDHI PRAYAS innovators move from drawing to prototype

Pre-incubation teams used the park’s prototyping and fab-lab support to test designs, validate assumptions and prepare for the next stage of product development.

NIDHI PRAYAS centre, Amingaon

12 Jan 2026

Thirteen ventures working from one campus

From genomics and enzymes to mead, medtech and fisheries, the park’s incubatee community now spans thirteen resident companies and institutions.

Technology Incubation Centre, Amingaon

5 Mar 2026

Student exposure visits and hands-on instrument training

School, college and university groups visited the park for guided laboratory sessions on analytical instruments, bioinformatics and applied biotechnology.

Guwahati Biotech Park campus, Amingaon

12 Sept 2024

Assam at Global Bio-India 2024

Assam participated as a State Partner at the fourth edition of Global Bio-India, where the park presented its facilities, BICFA, NIDHI PRAYAS and the State’s biotech policy.

Pragati Maidan, New Delhi

15 Jan 2025

BICFA biofloc and laboratory facility inaugurated

The Business Incubation Centre for Fisheries and Aquaculture opened its biofloc setup and laboratory, alongside a training manual on ornamental fish farming and aquarium setup.

BICFA, Guwahati Biotech Park

20 Jun 2024

Students explore the labs through exposure visits

Teams from Gauhati University, Gurucharan College, Tihu College and BBK College toured the instrumentation, greenhouse, cold room and incubation facilities through 2024.

Guwahati Biotech Park, Amingaon

31 Jul 2024

AROHAN students visit the park

Students from government schools in Darrang and Tinsukia explored the laboratories, instrumentation and BICFA’s fisheries incubation work during an AROHAN scheme visit.

Guwahati Biotech Park, Amingaon

1 Aug 2024

Visit to Khaisua Foods and Iluscube

The GBP team met founder Dhruba Jyoti Kalita to explore collaborations around ready-to-eat foods, ornamental fish and handcrafted planted aquariums.

Amingaon, Guwahati

1 Dec 2024

Fuloni named Best Environmental Impact Startup

Incubatee Fuloni was recognised at the TIA Awards 2024 for plantable, compostable and bioplastic alternatives to conventional horticulture pots.

Guwahati

1 Nov 2024

Medinovation reaches the National Bio Entrepreneurship finale

Park incubatee Medinovation Research and Consultancy reached the grand finale of the National Bio Entrepreneurship Competition and was shortlisted for a BioAngels investment proposal.

C-CAMP, Bengaluru
